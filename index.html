About the NBL

The Neutral Buoyancy Laboratory is part of the Sonny Carter Training Facility at NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston, Texas. This massive underwater training facility has been preparing astronauts for spacewalks since 1997.

While astronauts in the NBL aim for neutral buoyancy, they are not truly weightless in the water - they still feel weight in their suits, and water drag affects movement differently than the vacuum of space.

The NBL supports mission planning, procedure development, hardware verification, astronaut training, and refinement of time-critical operations for EVAs (spacewalks).
